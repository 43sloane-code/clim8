Why HK is harder

Intraday only works off the exact settlement station.

  • London settles on London City Airport (EGLC) — an airport that has an hourly archive → intraday works out of the box.
  • Hong Kong settles on the HKO Observatory (no hourly archive). The airport VHHH has hourly data but is a different station 25 km away → tested as a proxy, failed (~38 %).

So the accumulator logs the Observatory's own live feed hourly to build the missing record. Closed 3 ways: no HKO hourly API, ERA5 32 %, VHHH 38 %.

Open / next

  • Keep the HK Observatory hourly archive accumulating (the only path to HK intraday conviction).
  • When it has ~40 days: add an HK arm to intraday_ceiling, trained vs the authoritative hko_daily_extract abs-max, validated leak-free before serving.
  • Day-ahead point accuracy is at its ceiling — do not chase it (AIFS, spread, recency, warm-bias, pmf-mode all failed/bucket-neutral).
